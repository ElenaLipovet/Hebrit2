Privacy Policy

Hebrit 2 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Hebrit 2 is a demo app for learning Hebrew, shared with a small group of user testers. This page explains what information the app collects when you log in, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you log in with Google or Meta (Facebook), we receive only basic profile details from that account:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile picture, if you have one
  • an account ID that the sign-in service gives us

We never see or store your Google or Meta password.

How we use it

  • to log you in and keep you logged in
  • to remember your progress in the lessons
  • to contact you about the demo, if needed

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your login details on our behalf. You can read Firebase's privacy information.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all information about you at any time. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my Hebrit data", and we will delete it within 30 days.

You can also remove Hebrit's access from your account settings: in Google under "Third-party apps & services", or in Facebook under "Apps and Websites".

Children

Hebrit 2 is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
